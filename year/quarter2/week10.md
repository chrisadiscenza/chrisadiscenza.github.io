### <b>  Relations and Functions! </b>

• Day 1: Review the types of matchings

– One to one

– Many to one

– One to many

– Discuss common intuitive examples: sending mail, flying from city to city...

– Dicsuss how these examples are either one to one or many to one

– Emphasize that they are not one to many

– Define a function as one to one or many to one

– But a function is not many to one. That's all a function is. Simple right?

• Day 2: Inverse Functions

– How do we unsend a letter or reply? Or take a return flight?

– These are examples of undoing. This is one of the most important concepts in math. 

– Solving for $x$?

– When does this fail or is impossible?

• Day 3:Functions with Numbers
