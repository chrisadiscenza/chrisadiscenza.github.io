### <b>  Meet My Imaginary Friend! </b>

•Ask questions about squaring. Extend to imaginary numbers!

• Day 1:It all starts with the question: $x^{2}=-1$?

Is this a stupid question? 

– We said last week that squaring always yields a positive number. 

– That's true if you start with integers. But we are asking a different question here. 

– Once again we are asking a question about our current land of numbers that doesn't have an answer.

– Have students discuss what to do. We invent a new type of number that answers the question. 

– Call it $i$.
