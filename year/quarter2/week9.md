### <b>   Relations: Proportional and Inequality </b>
• Day 1: Relations between sets

– Relations as matchings, draw diagrams

– One to one

– Many to one

– One to many

– Many to many

– Discuss examples: students to chairs, flights between cities, sending letters...

– Discuss cardinality: students in chairs example

• Day 2: Cartesian Product Direct Proportional relations

– Look at all possible matchings (or pairings). This is the Cartesian Product of two sets.

– What is the cardinality of the Cartesian product?

– Relations as subsets of the Cartesian product.

• Day 3:Direct Proportional relations

– Cooking ingredients to number of servings

– Examples with numbers

– Plot examples on a graph

– Illustrate that a graph is a subset of the Cartesian product.

• Day 4: Inequalities

– Relation of older than or younger than

– Now with numbers: $x<y, x\leq y$

– Have students make plots of inequalities, shade regions.

– Then use Desmos for instant satisfaction.

• Day 5: Inverse Proportions

– Build intuition using examples.

– They should understand: big demoninator ~ small fraction

– And small demoninator ~ big fraction

– Discuss the extremes of these two situations.

– Plot examples of inverse proportionality.
