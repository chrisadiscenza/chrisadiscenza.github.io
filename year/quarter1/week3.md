### <b> Square Game </b>

• Day 1. Introduce the Square Game.

– Pass out square pieces of paper. Have the studeents label the corners with A, B, C, D clockwise around starting from the upper left corner.  Have kids decorate their squares so they cherish them. 

– Work together to demonstrate the rotations: $R_{0}, R_{1},R_{2}, R_{3}$

– There are only clockwise rotations. There are no counterclockwise roations in the Square Game. This is important because they will be asked how to "undo" a $ R_{1}$ rotation. Undo means to return the square to the starting orientation, $R_{0}$, with A, B and the top. They can achieve this with the $ R_{3}$ roation. 

– Perform rotation combinations and have students create a table of this.

• Day 2: Discuss patterns and symmetries in rotation table. Introduce flips of the square. Add flips to the table. Flip the square about the horizontal axis and label the back with letters: E,F,G,H. This extend the game to include horizontal and vertical axis flips, $F_{H},F_{V}$ along with flips about the left and right diagonals:  $D_{L},D_{R}$. All together the Square game has 8 pieces: 4 rotations and 4 flips. 

• Day 3: Fill out table with flips. Discuss properties of table. Note that order now matters. Example: socks before shoes.

• Day 4: Discuss identity, inverses (this is very important). Solve simple equations.

• Day 5: Solve harder equations. Discuss equations with no solutions. What do we do?
