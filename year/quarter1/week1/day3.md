#### <b> Set Game</b>

Have the students play the Set Game. Then have them make some observations. 

How many cards were left over at the end of the game? 

Would it be possible to end the game with zero left over cards? 

What's the most number of cards that can be leftover at the end of the game? How would you determine this?

As yo play, what is the largest array of cards that does not contain a Set?

Could you expand the game to include cards with a more shapes, more colors, more fillings, and more than 3 shapes on a cards? How many cards would you need to make a Set?

Have the students  discuss and write about this in their notebooks. Have them draw their ideas for an expanded game.
